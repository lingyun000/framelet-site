支持 / Support

中文

使用 Framelet 时遇到问题，请先检查 macOS「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」中相应的屏幕录制、辅助功能或麦克风授权。购买、续订、取消和退款由 Apple 管理，可从订阅管理开始。

一般功能问题可在公开问题反馈页描述 macOS 版本、Framelet 版本、操作步骤和实际结果。此页面需要 GitHub 账号，且内容公开。请勿上传私人截图、录屏、OCR 文本、日志原件、Apple 账户信息或付款凭证。如问题涉及敏感内容，先只描述现象并要求私密联系渠道。

隐私政策 · 使用条款

English

For app issues, first check the relevant Screen Recording, Accessibility, or Microphone permission in macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security. Apple manages purchases, renewals, cancellations, and refunds; start at subscription management.

For general issues, use the public issue form and include your macOS version, Framelet version, steps, and observed result. A GitHub account is required and posts are public. Do not upload private screenshots, recordings, OCR text, raw logs, Apple account details, or payment records. For a sensitive issue, describe the symptom only and request a private contact method.
